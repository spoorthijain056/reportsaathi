Many patients, particularly elderly individuals and people with limited medical knowledge or English-language proficiency, struggle to understand doctors' prescriptions and laboratory reports because they contain complex medical terminology, unfamiliar medicine names, dosage instructions, and diagnostic values. This lack of understanding can lead to medication misuse, confusion, unnecessary anxiety, and dependence on others, creating a need for an accessible, multilingual tool that explains medical documents in simple, patient-friendly language without replacing professional medical advice.
